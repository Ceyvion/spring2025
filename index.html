Preserving Our Musical Heritage

For 36 years, Afropop Worldwide has been documenting, preserving, and celebrating the rich musical traditions of Africa and the African diaspora. Our work connects communities, honors cultural legacies, and ensures these vital sounds continue to resonate with future generations.

This spring, we invite you to join us in sustaining this important mission through our giving campaign. Your support helps us preserve rare recordings, document emerging artists, and share these vibrant musical stories with the world.

Our Legacy

Since 1988, we've built the world's most comprehensive audio archive of African and diaspora music, connecting listeners to sounds that might otherwise be lost to time.

Our Vision

We're evolving our archival and educational efforts to meet the challenges of a digital world, ensuring these cultural treasures reach new audiences globally.

Supporting Afropop's Cultural Journey

Our campaign focuses on these key initiatives that will strengthen our ability to document, preserve, and share the rich musical heritage of Africa and its diaspora.

Cultural Storytelling Archive

Our Foundation

We've collected thousands of stories about the music that defines cultures across Africa and its diaspora. These stories deserve to be shared more widely and preserved for future generations.

Our Vision

  • 36 Years, 36 Stories: A curated collection highlighting one significant artist, tradition, or musical moment from each year of our history.
  • Rhythm Mapping Project: An interactive digital archive where visitors can explore musical connections across regions and time periods.
  • Artist Journey Documentation: In-depth profiles capturing how musicians develop their unique sounds and cultural significance.
  • Community Impact Stories: Personal narratives about how music has preserved cultural identity through challenging times.

"Music gives us a language beyond words to express our deepest cultural memories and shared experiences. These stories aren't just about sounds—they're about who we are."

Community Rhythm Collaborations

Our Foundation

Music has always been a participatory experience, bringing people together across generations and geographies. Our digital age offers new opportunities to connect through shared musical experiences.

Our Vision

  • Rhythm Share Project: An accessible way for anyone to record and share a simple beat that represents their cultural heritage.
  • Collaborative Sound Tapestry: A growing digital composition where participants contribute elements that blend into a global musical conversation.
  • Regional Rhythm Circles: Virtual community gatherings celebrating specific musical traditions with artist-led demonstrations.
  • Global Beat Celebration: A culminating virtual event where communities worldwide participate in a synchronized musical experience.

"When people participate in making music together, even digitally, they form connections that transcend language and geography. These collaborative experiences help preserve traditions while making them relevant to contemporary audiences."
